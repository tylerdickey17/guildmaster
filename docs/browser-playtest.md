[Play Contract Operations](https://contract-operations-playtest.primeangel2.chatgpt.site). **Current version0.3.8**; existing browser progress is preserved.

1. Choose **New playtest → Staffed starter** for the new opening experience. It starts with2,000 credits and a350-credit Upkeep invoice; later bare base+suite costs365/month. **Lean** starts with an empty300/month office. **Expansion test** gives Guild Level4,8,000 credits and four Level3 operatives to exercise purchases immediately; this is an artificial fixture.
2. Review the invoice. Pay selected bills or apply available cash; pauses concern month-to-month facility support only. Unpaid balances stay owed and unfunded services are unavailable.
3. Open Activity after any completed training choices. Every player gets first choice; the pool is PC count + Guild Level −1 with no personal cap.
4. New installations unlock at Guild Level 2; owned bases/outposts at Level 4. Use **Bases & upgrades** for property purchases, future bills, destination slots, installation staffing, chiefs and relocation. Buying a ready property costs one action and its advertised price; upgrades and chief appointments remain separate.
5. Secure a local contract and deploy an available squad with its three leads locked. Resolution stays in the same turn's Events, before the monthly event and Advancement.
6. Continue several monthly turns; compare idle overhead, construction spending, reserve coverage, retention and expansion. Copy/download notes to discuss actual decisions.

The site preserves0.3.1–0.3.3 saves without rewriting money or old rolls. Current paid intervals are not rebilled; no free preset installation is added to existing saves. To experience the new first invoice, start a new playtest after exporting any notes you want to retain.

## Finance and progression 0.3.6

Use **New playtest → Developing guild** for an artificial Level 2, 2,000-credit fixture. It starts without upgrades or invented income. Complete a contract and advance before applying for an unsecured loan; underwriting needs recorded receipts. Expansion test begins at Level 4 but also must earn actual client receipts.

Under **Bases & upgrades → Guild financing**, review unsecured (Level 2) or secured (Level 4) terms. A secured application requires an operational owned base; a lease or a property still awaiting possession cannot be pledged. Loan proceeds arrive on funding, after one shared action; repayments begin next Upkeep as itemized principal and interest. No approval roll. A refusal explains the precise level, arrears, reputation, collateral or service limit.

Prepay principal freely in Activity after curing that agreement's arrears. Missed balances remain owed, become Delinquent and then Default if uncured at the next boundary. Full seizure/restructuring is not simulated. Odd-level Guild Feats begin at 3; even levels unlock development or increase project capacity.
