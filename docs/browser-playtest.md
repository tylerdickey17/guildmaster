All-three-critical performance has optional GM guidance, with no automatic bonus. [Current rules and migration](prototype-values-v0.3.10.md).

6. Continue several monthly turns; compare idle overhead, construction spending, reserve coverage, retention and expansion. Copy/download notes to discuss actual decisions.

## Finance and progression 0.3.6

Use **New playtest → Developing guild** for an artificial Level 2, 2,000-credit fixture. It starts without upgrades or invented income. Complete a contract and advance before applying for an unsecured loan; underwriting needs recorded receipts. Expansion test begins at Level 4 but also must earn actual client receipts.

Under **Bases & upgrades → Guild financing**, review unsecured (Level 2) or secured (Level 4) terms. A secured application requires an operational owned base; a lease or a property still awaiting possession cannot be pledged. Loan proceeds arrive on funding, after one shared action; repayments begin next Upkeep as itemized principal and interest. No approval roll. A refusal explains the precise level, arrears, reputation, collateral or service limit.

Prepay principal freely in Activity after curing that agreement's arrears. Missed balances remain owed, become Delinquent and then Default if uncured at the next boundary. Full seizure/restructuring is not simulated. Odd-level Guild Feats begin at 3; even levels unlock development or increase project capacity.
