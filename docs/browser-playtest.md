# Browser playtest: opening season and expansion

[Play Contract Operations](https://contract-operations-playtest.primeangel2.chatgpt.site). **Current version0.3.5**; existing browser progress is preserved.

1. Choose **New playtest → Staffed starter** for the new opening experience. It starts with2,000 credits and a350-credit Upkeep invoice; later bare base+suite costs365/month. **Lean** starts with an empty300/month office. **Expansion test** gives Guild Level4,8,000 credits and four Level3 operatives to exercise purchases immediately; this is an artificial fixture.
2. Review the invoice. Pay selected bills or apply available cash; pauses concern month-to-month facility support only. Unpaid balances stay owed and unfunded services are unavailable.
3. Open Activity after any completed training choices. Every player gets first choice; the pool is PC count + Guild Level −1 with no personal cap.
4. Use **Bases & upgrades** for property purchases, future bills, destination slots, installation staffing, chiefs and relocation. Buying a ready property costs one action and its advertised price; upgrades and chief appointments remain separate.
5. Secure a local contract and deploy an available squad with its three leads locked. Resolution stays in the same turn's Events, before the monthly event and Advancement.
6. Continue several monthly turns; compare idle overhead, construction spending, reserve coverage, retention and expansion. Copy/download notes to discuss actual decisions.

The site preserves0.3.1–0.3.3 saves without rewriting money or old rolls. Current paid intervals are not rebilled; no free preset installation is added to existing saves. To experience the new first invoice, start a new playtest after exporting any notes you want to retain.

## Contract feedback patch

Choose Easy/Normal/Hard/Expert/Legendary on contract lead searches; Expansion test presents all five bands. New squads need three distinct eligible phase leads, minimum crew3. Review die+modifier=total against DC after every phase; the third waits for explicit continuation. Administrative rolls remain visible in the persistent result panel. Risk cards disclose recovery, charter repairs and a possible Missing operative. Expert/Legendary completion adds referrals, without free hiring. Already-deployed old jobs retain their frozen rules.
